Leaderboard privacy and recovery

Online play is optional. Offline play does not require registration or leaderboard uploads.

What is stored

The service stores your chosen public handle, an installation ID derived from a random credential, a hash of that credential, and your best replay and score for each official chart. Clients from v0.3.3 do not read a Windows MachineGuid or hardware identifier. Older clients used a hashed Windows-installation fingerprint, which remains stored for compatibility with existing handles. Replays contain timing, positions, pressure, device flags and calibration, not music or local file paths. Your private credential is locally protected with Windows DPAPI and is not stored on the server.

Public boards show handles, scores, accuracy, maximum combo and submission times. Server-scored means the replay matches the scoring rules, not that a human played it or that the player has a verified identity.

Retention and moderation

Best scores and reserved handles remain until operator deletion or moderation. There is no automatic inactivity deletion. Turning Online off stops new submissions but does not erase existing records. Private database backups rotate across the latest 14 snapshots; deleted data may remain in those backups until they rotate out. These backups are currently on the same server, not an off-site disaster-recovery copy.

Recovery and deletion

Handles are bound to a game installation/profile, not a physical machine or verified person. Keep your local online identity file: losing it does not automatically release your handle. Upgrading retains your credential, handle and scores. Knowing a public handle alone is not proof of ownership. For recovery, deletion or moderation requests, contact the operator through the release repository. Do not post private credentials, identity files, installation IDs or other personal information in a public issue. Recovery requires private ownership verification and is not guaranteed.
